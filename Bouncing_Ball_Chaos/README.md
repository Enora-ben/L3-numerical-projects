# Bouncing Ball Chaos

This numerical project follows an experimental study of chaos in a bouncing ball system.

The aim is to model the motion of the ball as accurately as possible and investigate the different dynamical regimes and the transition to chaotic behavior.

The parameters of the oscillating platform, including its frequency and amplitude, are varied to study their influence on the ball's trajectory. The extreme sensitivity to initial conditions is highlighted by comparing trajectories corresponding to very similar initial velocities.

The project also addresses the computational challenges associated with large amounts of simulation data and aims to optimize the numerical calculations and data processing.

## Topics

- Classical mechanics
- Nonlinear dynamics
- Chaos
- Sensitivity to initial conditions
- Numerical simulation
- Data analysis
- Computational optimization
